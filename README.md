### Abstract
Co-located Photovoltaic (PV) and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) face substantial financial exposure in modern deregulated power markets due to high day-ahead forecast uncertainty and volatile real-time imbalance settlement mechanisms (e.g., the German reBAP system). This paper proposes a two-stage Stochastic Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) formulation incorporating Conditional Value-at-Risk ($\text{CVaR}_\alpha$, $\alpha=0.95$) and piecewise linear Depth-of-Discharge (DoD) degradation costs constrained by manufacturer warranty boundaries.
